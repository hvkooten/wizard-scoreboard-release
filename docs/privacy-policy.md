## Data stored on your device

Groups, player names, games, scores, highscores and settings are stored **only on your device**. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

## Advertising (Android and iOS)

The free version shows ads from **Google AdMob**. To show ads and measure their performance, Google may collect:

- your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, only with your permission on iOS);
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
- device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version;
- interactions with ads.

On first use you are asked for consent where required by law (for example in the EU/EEA and UK). You can change your choice at any time in the consent form or in your device settings. On iOS, tracking is only used if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Google's use of data is described at <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

## In-app purchase

You can buy "Remove ads" through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is fully handled by Google or Apple; we never receive your payment details. The app only checks with the store whether you own the purchase.

## Bug reports

When **you** choose to report a bug, the app prepares an email or a text to copy. It contains the app version, device model, operating system version, language and, if available, recent crash information. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. We only use reports to fix problems.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

## Your rights

Because we do not store personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to view, correct or delete. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy at <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page.
